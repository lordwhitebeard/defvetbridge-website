How Evocatus keeps your records safe

Evocatus was built by a veteran, for veterans. Your service and medical records are some of the most personal things you own, and every part of Evocatus is designed around protecting them.

Your records stay in Australia

  • Stored in Australia. Your files are kept on our own server in Queensland.
  • Read in Australia. Documents are read using Amazon Web Services (AWS) data centres in Sydney and Melbourne only. Evocatus is geographically locked to Australia: if anything attempts to send a document overseas, it is blocked.
  • Backed up in Australia. Every night a locked (encrypted) copy is saved with AWS in Sydney. Only Evocatus holds the key, so nobody else can open it.

Your name stays with you

  • Your details are removed before the AI reads anything. Your name, date of birth, service and DVA file numbers, address, phone and email are taken off each page first. The AI sees your medical history, not who you are.
  • Your records never train AI. They are read to help you, and that is all.
  • AI can be switched off instantly. One switch stops all AI reading, and Evocatus keeps working.

Only the right people get in

  • By invitation only. Nobody can sign themselves up.
  • No new password to remember or steal. You sign in with the Google or Microsoft account you already have.
  • Advocates see only their own veterans. An advocate can see your records only when they are linked to your account.

Your records are kept apart

  • Walled off from everyone else. Every veteran's records are kept separate inside the system itself.
  • When in doubt, it shows nothing. If Evocatus is ever unsure whose records are being asked for, it shows nothing at all.
  • Checked every time. This is tested automatically every time Evocatus is updated.

Nothing happens without a record

  • You agree first. Before your first upload we ask for your agreement, and keep a record of exactly what you agreed to.
  • Your originals are never changed. Your documents are kept exactly as you gave them.
  • Changes are recorded. Account changes, corrections and removals are all logged.
  • You can leave at any time. Ask us to delete your account and everything is removed. You have 30 days to change your mind.

Checked and watched, every day

  • More than 4,100 checks must pass before any update goes live.
  • A safety copy is made before any change to how your records are stored.
  • Software is checked for known security problems with every update.
  • Watched around the clock. If something goes wrong, we are alerted straight away.
  • A plan for the worst day. If your information is ever put at risk, we have a written plan for what to do, and we will tell you when Australian law requires it.
  • Regular security reviews, with every problem found tracked until it is fixed.

Your information is never for sale

  • No selling, no advertising.
  • A secure connection. Your browser and Evocatus talk over an encrypted connection: look for the padlock in your address bar.
  • Try it free, with no AI. The free preview sends nothing to AI.

For advocates and firms: Evocatus Professional

Evocatus Professional runs on the firm's own computer, in its own office. Its storage is fully locked (encrypted), so client records never leave the building.
